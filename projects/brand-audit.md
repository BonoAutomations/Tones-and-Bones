Conduct a full brand audit to identify gaps, inconsistencies, and opportunities before expanding to new markets. Exit with a clear, updated brand identity and positioning.

## Audit Checklist

### Digital Presence
- [ ] Website: headline, bio, proof points, CTAs aligned to ICP?
- [ ] LinkedIn: banner, headline, About, featured posts updated?
- [ ] X (@Bono_1033 + @AiSales2782): bio, pinned post, recent content consistent?
- [ ] Whop store: product descriptions match current positioning?
- [ ] Stripe products: copy matches brand voice?

### Messaging
- [ ] Is there a consistent tagline across all platforms?
- [ ] Does every touchpoint start with the client's problem (not your credentials)?
- [ ] Are there any outdated claims, awards, or statistics?
- [ ] Does the brand story feel authentic or rehearsed?

### Competitive Position
- [ ] Who are the top 3 competitors in your primary market?
- [ ] How do you differ from each?
- [ ] Is the differentiation visible in your marketing?
- [ ] Are you being priced appropriately for your position?

### Content & Voice
- [ ] Is the content calendar running? (CashClaw auto-posts 3x/day when X API funded)
- [ ] Is the content addressing ICP pain points?
- [ ] Are testimonials and case studies current?
- [ ] Is there a content gap between what you produce and what competitors produce?
