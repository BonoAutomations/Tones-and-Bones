# New Client Acquisition

A structured project for every prospective client that enters the pipeline. Open this project when a new qualified lead makes contact. Close it when the contract is signed or the lead is formally declined.

## Stages

### Stage 1: Lead Qualification
- [ ] Identify lead source (referral / social / event / outreach)
- [ ] Score the lead using the Ideal Client Profile framework (`/ideal-client-profile`)
- [ ] Determine trigger event: what happened in their life that brought them here?
- [ ] Decision: qualified (continue) or not the right fit (gracefully redirect)

### Stage 2: First Touchpoint
- [ ] Respond within 4 hours during business hours
- [ ] Tone: warm, confident, curious — not eager
- [ ] Ask one thoughtful qualifying question
- [ ] Propose next step: consultation, not a call
- [ ] Log response in CRM with notes on communication style

### Stage 3: Pre-Consultation
- [ ] Send pre-consultation questionnaire
- [ ] Research the lead (LinkedIn, website, mutual connections)
- [ ] Prepare one insight specific to their situation
- [ ] Confirm time, format, and expectations

### Stage 4: Consultation
- [ ] Listen 70%, speak 30%
- [ ] Understand their situation before offering anything
- [ ] Do NOT discuss price during this conversation
- [ ] Close with: "I'd like to put something together for you — give me 48 hours"

### Stage 5: Proposal
- [ ] Send bespoke proposal within 48 hours
- [ ] Format reflects brand standard (not a template dump)
- [ ] Include: situation as understood, transformation offered, investment, next steps
- [ ] Follow up once, elegantly, if no response within 5 business days

### Stage 6: Close or Decline
- [ ] Contract sent digitally (DocuSign / HoneyBook)
- [ ] Invoice or deposit link sent immediately upon signature
- [ ] Hand off to Client Experience Director for onboarding
- [ ] OR: gracefully decline with a referral to someone who may be a better fit

## Notes

Log all communications, objections, and outcomes in CRM. Patterns across multiple projects inform quarterly sales funnel reviews.
