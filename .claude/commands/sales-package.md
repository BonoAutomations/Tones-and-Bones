# /sales-package — Generate a Complete Sales Package

Create a full sales package for the product, service, or offering described in $ARGUMENTS.

A sales package has three components: Narrative, Talking Points, and Positioning. Deliver all three.

---

## Step 1: Extract the Narrative Arc

Identify:
- The core emotional journey (wonder → desire → decision)
- The protagonist (the ideal client — who they are, what they want, what they fear)
- The transformation (before state → after state)
- The unique proof point (what makes this offer irreplaceable)

## Step 2: Determine the Emotional Register

Select the dominant tone:
- **Wonder** — For launches, debut collections, or category-defining moments
- **Determination** — For performance-driven or results-oriented positioning
- **Hope** — For aspirational lifestyle or transformation narratives

## Step 3: Deliver the Three Assets

### 3a. Narrative (8–12 points, conversational first-person voice)

Write in the voice of the ideal client experiencing the transformation. Each point is one beat in the journey. No bullet points — flowing numbered prose.

1. [Opening — set the scene, the longing, the gap]
2. ...
...
[Closing — the arrival, the transformation complete]

### 3b. Talking Points (for sales conversations)

Five to seven crisp, memorable lines a sales professional can deliver naturally in conversation. These are not features — they are conversation starters and proof points.

- **[Hook]:** [One sentence that opens a conversation]
- **[Differentiator]:** [What no one else can say]
- **[Social Proof]:** [Evidence of the claim]
- **[Objection Handler]:** [Pre-empt the most common objection]
- **[Call to Next Step]:** [Low-pressure invitation to continue]

### 3c. Positioning Statement

One paragraph (3–5 sentences) suitable for website hero copy, deck cover, or email introduction. It captures:
- Who this is for
- What it delivers
- Why it's the only logical choice for that person

---

## Quality Check

Before delivering:
- [ ] Every word earns its place — no filler
- [ ] Narrative flows — each point connects to the next
- [ ] Talking points are memorable, not corporate
- [ ] Positioning statement could run without editing
- [ ] Tones and Bones are both present
