# /campaign-brief — Create a Campaign Brief with ISA

Create a full campaign brief for the initiative described in $ARGUMENTS. The brief includes strategic framing, an embedded ISA, and a delivery checklist.

## Campaign Brief: [Campaign Name]

**Date:** [today]  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** [infer or ask]

---

### Strategic Context

**Business Objective:**  
What business outcome does this campaign serve? (e.g., pipeline, revenue, retention, brand)

**Target Audience:**  
Who specifically are we trying to reach? Include psychographic detail, not just demographics.

**Campaign Hypothesis:**  
Complete this: "If we [do X], then [audience Y] will [do Z] because [reason]."

---

### ISA — Campaign Ideal State

**Problem:** What's the current gap this campaign closes?

**Vision:** What does success look like at campaign end?

**Goal:** One sentence. "This campaign succeeds when ___."

**Criteria:**
- [ ] [Measurable outcome 1]
- [ ] [Measurable outcome 2]
- [ ] [Measurable outcome 3]

**Out of Scope:** What are we explicitly not doing in this campaign?

**Constraints:** Budget, timeline, brand guardrails, channel limits.
