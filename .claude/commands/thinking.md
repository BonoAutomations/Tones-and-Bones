# /thinking — Structured Multi-Mode Analysis

Run structured analysis on the topic in $ARGUMENTS. Select the appropriate thinking mode based on the nature of the problem.

---

## Mode Selection

Choose the mode that fits the task:

| Mode | Use When |
|------|----------|
| **FirstPrinciples** | You're questioning a foundational assumption or rebuilding from scratch |
| **Council** | You need multiple expert perspectives to stress-test a decision |
| **RedTeam** | You need adversarial challenge to a plan or strategy |
| **RootCause** | Something failed and you need to diagnose why |
| **Systems** | You're dealing with interconnected variables and second-order effects |
| **Depth** | You want to go progressively deeper into a topic across multiple lenses |

---

## Council Mode (default for strategy decisions)

Convene a council of 4 relevant experts for the Tones-and-Bones context. Each expert speaks from their distinct perspective.

**Council Members (adapt to context):**
- **The Brand Strategist** — Luxury brand equity, positioning, long-term perception
- **The Performance Marketer** — ROI, attribution, conversion, measurement
- **The Sales Leader** — Pipeline, relationship dynamics, close rates, buyer psychology
- **The Client** — The ideal customer's actual experience, concerns, and desires

**Council Process:**
1. State the question to the council clearly.
2. Each member speaks: their perspective, their concern, their recommendation.
3. Areas of agreement across all four members.
4. Areas of productive tension (genuine disagreement).
5. Synthesis: what decision emerges from the full picture?

---

## Red Team Mode

**Target:** [The plan, strategy, or artifact being stress-tested]

**Red Team Mandate:** Find every way this fails. Assume it will fail — your job is to find out how.

1. **Most Likely Failure Mode:** What's the #1 way this goes wrong?
2. **Strategic Blind Spots:** What are we not seeing?
3. **Adversarial Exploitation:** If a competitor saw this, how would they use it against us?
4. **Assumption Failures:** Which assumptions, if wrong, sink the whole thing?
5. **Verdict:** Is this plan robust enough, and what are the critical fixes?

---

## Root Cause Analysis

**Symptom:** [What went wrong]

**5 Whys:**
1. Why did [symptom] occur? → [Answer]
2. Why did [Answer 1] occur? → [Answer]
3. Why did [Answer 2] occur? → [Answer]
4. Why did [Answer 3] occur? → [Answer]
5. Why did [Answer 4] occur? → **[Root Cause]**

**Systemic Fix:** What change prevents this root cause from recurring?  
**Symptomatic Fix:** What stops the bleeding right now?
