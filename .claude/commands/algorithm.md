# /algorithm — Run the 7-Phase Algorithm

Apply the Algorithm to the problem or task described in $ARGUMENTS.

Work through all seven phases in sequence. Show your reasoning at each step. Do not skip phases.

---

## OBSERVE
Gather all available context about the problem.
- What do we currently know?
- What is the current state?
- What data or context is missing?
- Who are the stakeholders?

## THINK
Analyze deeply before acting.
- What are the root causes or core drivers?
- What constraints apply (brand, legal, budget, timeline)?
- What are the risks and dependencies?
- What analogies or precedents exist in luxury sales/marketing?

## PLAN
Design the execution path.
- What are the ordered steps?
- What are the decision points?
- What could go wrong, and what's the contingency?
- What does success look like (tie to ISA criteria if one exists)?

## BUILD
Create the output.
- Produce the deliverable described in the plan.
- Apply Tones-and-Bones standards: elevated voice, precise structure.
- Every artifact must have both Tones (narrative, voice) and Bones (structure, data).

## EXECUTE
Deliver or deploy the output.
- Present the final artifact.
- Flag anything requiring human approval before going live.

## VERIFY
Validate against the goal.
- Does this meet the success criteria from the PLAN phase?
- What's the gap between this output and the ideal state?
- What would need to change to close that gap?

## LEARN
Capture insights for the memory system.
- What worked well in this process?
- What would you do differently?
- Update `.claude/MEMORY/LEARNING.md` with any new patterns.
