## Brand

**Brand Name:** Tones-and-Bones  
**Category:** Luxury Sales and Marketing Automation  
**Core Promise:** White-glove precision at scale  
**Brand Archetype:** The Sage + The Ruler (wisdom + authority)

**Brand Voice Attributes:**
- Elevated (never casual, never corporate)
- Precise (every word earns its place)
- Warm (human, never transactional)
- Authoritative (earned, not asserted)

**What Tones-and-Bones Is NOT:**
- Pushy or urgent-sounding
- Feature-led (always story-first)
- Generic or templated in feel
- Volume-driven

## Luxury Market Principles

- Luxury is defined by what it excludes, not what it includes
- The price is never the message — the value is
- Scarcity (real or perceived) drives desire
- The relationship precedes the sale in luxury; sometimes by years
- Aesthetic consistency is non-negotiable — a single off-brand touchpoint breaks trust
- The client buys the story they're able to tell about themselves
