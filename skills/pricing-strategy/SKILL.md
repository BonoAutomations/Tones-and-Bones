# Skill: Pricing Strategy

**Command:** `/pricing-strategy`
**Category:** Revenue Design
**Hermes Tag:** `revenue.pricing`

---

## Purpose

Design a premium pricing structure with psychological anchor framing for digital products, services, or listing commissions. Luxury pricing is about perceived value architecture — the right price point in the right context makes the offer feel inevitable.

---

## When to Use

- Setting prices for Whop products or Stripe services
- Negotiating commission structure for a luxury listing
- Raising prices on existing products
- Building the conversion ladder in the ASAM product catalog

---

## How to Activate

Provide Claude with:
1. **Product/service** — What are you pricing?
2. **Current price** — What do you charge now (if anything)?
3. **Competitors' prices** — What do alternatives cost?
4. **Client outcome value** — What is the financial/emotional value of the result?
5. **Objections** — What do prospects say when they don't buy?

---

## Outputs

1. **Pricing Architecture** — Entry / Mid / Premium / VIP tier structure with rationale
2. **Anchor Product** — Which product makes everything else look reasonably priced?
3. **Price Framing Scripts** — 3 ways to present the price that shift perception
4. **Objection Responses** — 5 common price objections + responses
5. **Urgency Levers** — Legitimate scarcity and urgency mechanisms (no fake timers)
6. **Upsell Timing** — When and how to introduce the next tier
7. **Price Increase Roadmap** — Staged increases over 6–12 months

## Example Prompt

```
/pricing-strategy

Product: GoHighLevel automation setup service
Current price: $500 flat
Competitors: Agencies charge $1,500–$3,000; freelancers $200–$400
Client outcome: 10+ hours/week saved, pipeline never goes cold
Objections: "I can figure it out myself", "That's a lot for software setup"
```
