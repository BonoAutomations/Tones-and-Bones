# Skill: Luxury Positioning

**Command:** `/luxury-positioning`
**Category:** Brand Strategy
**Hermes Tag:** `brand.positioning`

---

## Purpose

Define a differentiated, premium market position for a luxury real estate agent, team, or property. Luxury positioning is about perceived exclusivity, emotional resonance, and a defensible niche — not just high price points.

---

## When to Use

- New agent entering luxury market
- Rebranding from mid-market to luxury
- Preparing a flagship listing for maximum price realization
- Building a premium digital presence (website, social, Whop store)

---

## How to Activate

Provide Claude with:
1. **Subject** — Who/what is being positioned? (agent name, brokerage, property address)
2. **Market** — Geography and price tier (e.g., "San Diego coastal, $2M–$8M")
3. **Current differentiators** — What already exists? (awards, niche expertise, network)
4. **Target client** — Who is the ideal buyer/seller? (wealth source, lifestyle, values)
5. **Competitors** — 2–3 agents/brands they need to differentiate from

---

## Outputs

1. **Positioning Statement** — single sentence: "For [target], [brand] is the [category] that [unique benefit] because [proof]."
2. **3 Brand Differentiators** — specific, provable, emotionally resonant
3. **Messaging Pillars** — 3 content/communication themes
4. **Brand Personality Attributes** — 5 adjectives that define tone and style
5. **Positioning Don'ts** — what to avoid saying/doing that erodes the position

---

## Example Prompt

```
/luxury-positioning

Subject: Bono G, independent luxury agent
Market: San Diego coastal + inland estates, $1.5M–$10M
Differentiators: AI-powered market analysis, 15yr RE background, tech-forward
Target: Business owners and tech executives relocating to SoCal
Competitors: Compass agents, Pacific Sotheby's, traditional RE teams
```

---

## Notes

- Lead with *exclusivity* and *expertise*, not just price
- Reference the ASAM brand: "precision, automation excellence, data-driven results"
- Every differentiator must be *provable* — avoid vague luxury claims
