# Skill: Referral Network

**Command:** `/referral-network`
**Category:** Growth Architecture
**Hermes Tag:** `growth.referrals`

---

## Purpose

Build a structured referral and affiliate partner ecosystem that generates consistent, warm, pre-qualified leads — without cold outreach or ad spend.

---

## When to Use

- Launching Phase 1 (Lock San Diego) — first 10 customers
- Building GHL contact database of potential referral partners
- Creating a Whop affiliate program or referral incentive structure
- Activating the Artemis (Researcher) lead enrichment pipeline

---

## How to Activate

Provide Claude with:
1. **Your specialty** — What specific outcomes do you deliver?
2. **Client profile** — Who do you serve? (from `/ideal-client-profile`)
3. **Current referral sources** — Who sends you business today?
4. **Desired referral volume** — How many/month would change your business?
5. **What you can offer** — Time, reciprocal referrals, rev share, gift cards?

---

## Outputs

1. **Partner Category Map** — 8–12 types of referral partners with rationale
2. **Top 5 Priority Partners** — Most likely to send ideal clients, ranked
3. **Outreach Templates** — Email + LinkedIn DM for each partner type
4. **Incentive Structure** — What to offer (ethical, non-commission-sharing options)
5. **Partner Onboarding Flow** — How to educate a partner so they refer correctly
6. **CRM Tagging System** — GHL tags + pipeline stage for partner management
7. **Referral Thank-You Ritual** — How to acknowledge and reinforce referral behavior
8. **90-Day Activation Plan** — Week-by-week partner cultivation steps

---

## Priority Partner Categories for Luxury RE + ASAM

| Partner Type | Why They Send Referrals | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage brokers (jumbo/private) | Same affluent buyer | Reciprocal referral |
| Estate attorneys | Selling inherited property | Educational content |
| CPAs / Wealth managers | Investment property, 1031 | Tax-angle content |
| Relocation specialists | Corporate relocations to SoCal | Referral fee |
| Interior designers | New buyers need staging/reno | Partnership package |
| Concierge doctors / private clubs | Ultra-HNW network | Event co-hosting |
| Tech startup ecosystem (SD/LA) | Pre-IPO exec housing | LinkedIn content |

## Example Prompt

```
/referral-network

Specialty: Luxury seller representation + AI marketing automation
Client: Tech execs, business owners, $2M–$8M San Diego
Current referral sources: 2 past clients, 1 mortgage broker
Desired volume: 3 warm leads/month
Can offer: Reciprocal referrals, co-branded content, rev share on digital products
```
