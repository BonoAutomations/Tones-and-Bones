# Skill: Brand Story

**Command:** `/brand-story`
**Category:** Content & Narrative
**Hermes Tag:** `content.brand-story`

---

## Purpose

Craft a compelling, authentic brand narrative that communicates who you are, why you exist, and why clients should trust you — all without sounding like a generic real estate bio.

---

## When to Use

- Writing an About page or LinkedIn summary
- Recording an intro video
- Pitching a listing presentation
- Onboarding a new Whop member or Stripe subscriber
- Building the CashClaw `personality.customInstructions`

---

## How to Activate

Provide Claude with:
1. **Origin** — Why did you get into luxury real estate or this business?
2. **Turning point** — What moment changed how you work?
3. **Core belief** — What do you believe about how clients should be treated?
4. **Proof** — 2–3 specific client outcomes or deals that exemplify your belief
5. **Future** — Where are you taking this?

---

## Outputs

1. **The Origin Story** — 150-word narrative (honest, not hype)
2. **The Core Belief Statement** — 1–2 sentences defining your philosophy
3. **The Mission Line** — 10-word purpose statement
4. **3 Brand Voice Examples:**
   - Email opening line
   - LinkedIn headline
   - Instagram bio
5. **Values List** — 5 non-negotiable values with one-line descriptions
6. **The Anti-Story** — What you stand against (just as important as what you stand for)

---

## Example Prompt

```
/brand-story

Origin: Left corporate tech sales after watching family lose a home in 2009
Turning point: First client who cried after closing — realized this is real
Core belief: Real estate is the biggest financial decision most people make; it deserves actual expertise, not a smooth pitch
Proof: $4.2M Del Mar sale in 9 days; helped relocating family find off-market before school year; saved investor $80K with pre-inspection negotiation
Future: Build the most automated, data-driven luxury team in San Diego
```

---

## Notes

- Emotion > credentials — lead with the human story
- Avoid clichés: "passionate about real estate", "hard worker", "client-first"
- The brand story should feel like the *reason* for the technology, not separate from it
