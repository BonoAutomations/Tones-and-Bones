# Skill: Ideal Client Profile

**Command:** `/ideal-client-profile`
**Category:** Lead Strategy
**Hermes Tag:** `leads.icp`

---

## Purpose

Build a precise Ideal Client Profile (ICP) for luxury real estate. An ICP defines exactly who you serve best, allowing every marketing dollar and sales hour to be directed at buyers and sellers most likely to convert and refer.

---

## When to Use

- Starting a new luxury farm area
- Before launching an ad campaign or Whop product
- Updating GHL CRM lead scoring criteria
- Building the CashClaw AI lead scoring prompt

---

## How to Activate

Provide Claude with:
1. **Service type** — Buyer rep, seller rep, investor acquisitions, or all?
2. **Geography** — Primary markets
3. **Past best clients** — 3 descriptors of your top 3 past clients
4. **Problems they come with** — What triggered them to seek help?
5. **Outcomes they want** — What does success look like to them?

---

## Outputs

1. **ICP Name** — A memorable persona name (e.g., "The Coastal Relocator")
2. **Demographics** — Age, income range, wealth source, family status
3. **Psychographics** — Values, identity, how they make decisions
4. **Pain Points** — Top 3 problems they need solved
5. **Buying Triggers** — What life event or moment causes them to act now?
6. **Where to Find Them** — Channels, communities, platforms, referral sources
7. **What They're NOT** — Anti-ICP definition to filter bad-fit leads
8. **CashClaw Scoring Signal** — Keyword/context triggers for AI lead scoring

---

## Example Prompt

```
/ideal-client-profile

Service: Luxury seller representation
Geography: La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe
Past best clients: Tech founder selling pre-IPO, retired couple downsizing estate, remote exec relocating
Problems: Need fast close, maximum net proceeds, private (no public listing)
Outcomes: Clean transaction, no drama, fair price, fast
```

---

## Notes

- The anti-ICP is as important as the ICP — saves time and margins
- Feed the ICP outputs directly into the CashClaw lead scoring system prompt
- Revisit every 90 days — ICPs shift with market conditions
