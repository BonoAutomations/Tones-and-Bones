# Skill: Client Experience

**Command:** `/client-experience`
**Category:** Service Design
**Hermes Tag:** `service.client-experience`

---

## Purpose

Map and systematize the white-glove client experience from first contact to post-close — so every client gets the luxury treatment, consistently, even when the agent is not personally available.

---

## When to Use

- Onboarding a new buyer or seller
- Building GHL automation sequences for client communication
- Creating service SOPs for a team
- Reducing churn on the Monthly Retainer subscription

---

## How to Activate

Provide Claude with:
1. **Transaction type** — Buying, selling, or retainer service?
2. **Price range** — Directly impacts expected service level
3. **Current touchpoints** — What do you currently do for clients?
4. **Client complaints** — What do they say when dissatisfied?
5. **Best experience ever** — Describe a time a client was blown away

---

## Outputs

1. **Journey Map** — 8–12 touchpoints from inquiry → close → post-close
2. **Service Rituals** — 5 repeatable, signature moments of delight
3. **Surprise-and-Delight Plan** — 3 unexpected gestures at key emotional moments
4. **Communication Cadence** — Frequency + channel (email, SMS, call, video) per stage
5. **GHL Automation Triggers** — Which touchpoints can be automated vs. must be personal
6. **NPS Improvement Plan** — 3 changes likely to raise referral rate
7. **Off-boarding Sequence** — How to transition from close → referral relationship

---

## Luxury Service Standards

At a minimum for $1M+ transactions:
- Response within 1 hour during business hours
- Weekly status update (automated ok, but personalized content)
- Day-of-close personal call
- 30-day post-close check-in
- 90-day market update (can be automated via CashClaw content brief)

---

## Example Prompt

```
/client-experience

Transaction: Luxury seller, $2.5M coastal listing
Current touchpoints: Initial call, listing agreement, occasional email updates, close
Complaints: "Didn't hear from you for 2 weeks during escrow"
Best experience: Hand-delivered closing gift with personalized market report for their next purchase
```
